about

A matcha & coffee-fueled product designer.

the short version

From front-end code to product design, and back again.

My journey into product design started during an internship as a front-end web developer. I went in thinking I might become the “cool programmer” I always imagined myself being, but along the way, I discovered that I was more interested in designing the experience than writing the code.

That curiosity led me to UI/UX, and I’ve been designing digital products ever since.

Today, I design websites, mobile apps, and digital products. And somehow, the dream of coding came back around. I’m now vibe coding and actually enjoying it, proving that maybe I just needed to find my own way of doing it.

These days, you’ll probably find me designing with a cup of coffee or matcha nearby.
