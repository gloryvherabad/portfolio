04 Pachamamas

Skincare told by the person who makes it.

An online shop for small-batch botanical skincare, told by the woman who makes it.

Role
Website design
Team
Chayland Design
Platform
E-commerce website
Focus
Brand story, shopping flow
Live site
thepachamamas.com

A The brief

Pachamamas is a small-batch botanical skincare brand started by Taryn after her children were born. Herbs are slow-infused for weeks, and most formulas use five ingredients or fewer. The shop needed to sell, and still feel like it came from her hands.

B The challenge

“Clean beauty” sites all start to look the same: white space, thin type, stock photos. Pachamamas had real photography and a real founder story. The design had to put those first and still make shopping simple.

C Key decisions

What I designed, and why.

  1. 01 Decision

    Founder first

    The hero leads with “Skincare rooted in nature and motherhood”, followed straight away by Taryn’s story. People buy from a person before they buy a product.

  2. 02 Decision

    Ingredients you can see

    Instead of a long INCI list, the five key ingredients are shown as simple objects: beeswax, shea butter, chamomile, jojoba and castor oil.

  3. 03 Decision

    Answer the worried questions

    The FAQ covers what parents ask most, like pregnancy and use on children, next to real photography. Reassurance sits where hesitation happens.

D On mobile

Designed for the phone first.

Most visitors arrive on a phone, so every section was designed to read and work at small sizes first.

E Outcome

What shipped

  • An earthy palette of cream, olive and clay taken from the brand’s photography, not a template.
  • A best-seller carousel that gets shoppers to products in one scroll.
  • A story-led homepage that keeps the founder’s voice from hero to footer.

If I kept going

I’d add short ritual guides to each product page, showing how and when to use it, to help first-time buyers choose.
