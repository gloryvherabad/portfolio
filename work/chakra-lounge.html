02 Chakra Lounge

A calmer way into everyday ritual.

A calmer, plain-language way into chakra-based self-care, built to grow into an app.

Role
Website redesign
Team
Chayland Design
Platform
Responsive website
Focus
Content structure, visual identity
Live site
chakra-lounge.com

A The brief

Chakra Lounge offers chakra-inspired self-care: guides, rituals, meditations and recipes, with an app on the way that connects people to trusted practitioners. The site needed to welcome curious beginners and also recruit practitioners.

B The challenge

Wellness content can feel vague or mystical, which makes newcomers bounce. The redesign had to give people a clear first step, explain each chakra in plain words, and set up two waitlists without confusing either audience.

C Key decisions

What I designed, and why.

  1. 01 Decision

    One clear first step

    The hero has one job: take the chakra quiz. Six short questions point to the chakra that needs attention, giving a beginner a personal place to start instead of a menu of everything.

  2. 02 Decision

    A path, not a library

    “How Chakra Lounge works” lays out four steps: take the quiz, learn your chakra, try a small ritual, go deeper. It turns a content library into a journey.

  3. 03 Decision

    Seven chakras, one visual index

    Each chakra gets a colour-coded card so people can browse by feeling, then read a short plain-language guide.

  4. 04 Decision

    Two audiences, two doors

    The app waitlist splits early access for members from applications for practitioners, so each group sees an action written for them.

D On mobile

Designed for the phone first.

Most visitors arrive on a phone, so every section was designed to read and work at small sizes first.

E Outcome

What shipped

  • A quiz-first homepage that gives beginners a personal starting point.
  • A plain-language structure that connects guides, rituals and the upcoming app.
  • A bolder visual identity that feels grounded rather than generic “spa”.

If I kept going

I’d track how many quiz takers continue to a guide and a ritual, and tune the quiz results page around that hand-off.
