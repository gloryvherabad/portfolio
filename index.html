01 How I work

AI gets a product to good. Design gets it to right.

I design websites, mobile apps and web products, and I build a lot of them myself with AI. It lets me test real ideas in days instead of weeks.

The speed is the easy part. The work is deciding what to cut, what to say first, and how each screen should feel in someone’s hand. That part still needs a person who cares.

AI first draft After design

The AI draft reads “Revolutionize your pool experience” with a purple gradient, emoji, two competing buttons and vague claims. The designed version reads “Crystal clear pools. Zero hassle.”, explains what is included, shows the starting price and has one clear button.

  • Specific beats impressive

    “Every chemical included” tells a homeowner more than any “cutting-edge solution”.

  • One clear next step

    One button with a real verb. A second button just splits the decision.

  • Answer the real question

    People want to know the price. Showing it early builds trust faster than adjectives.

05 Process

I start with the problem, not the screen.

  1. 01

    Understand

    What are we solving, and for whom? I talk to the team, read the reviews and look at what people actually do.

  2. 02

    Simplify

    I organize the content and remove what doesn’t help someone decide or act.

  3. 03

    Design

    Clear screens, interactions and a visual system that fits the brand, not a template.

  4. 04

    Refine

    I test, look for friction, and polish spacing, copy and states until nothing feels accidental.

  5. 05

    Build

    I work with developers, or build it myself with AI, so the shipped product matches the design.
