01 The short version

From front-end code to design, and back again.

  • RoleUI/UX Product Designer
  • StudioChayland Design
  • DesignsWebsites, apps, web apps
  • Builds withFigma, AI, a lot of tea
  • Based inThe Philippines, remote

My path into product design started with an internship as a front-end developer. I went in thinking I’d become the cool programmer I always pictured. Along the way I noticed I cared more about how the experience felt than about the code behind it.

That curiosity took me to UI/UX, and I’ve been designing digital products ever since.

Then the coding came back around. With AI I now vibe code a lot of what I design, from quick prototypes to real pages. It turns out I just needed my own way in.

What hasn’t changed is the part I love most: taking something fast and rough and making it clean, clear and intentional. AI is a great collaborator. It still needs someone with taste in the room.

03 What I believe

Good design isn’t only how it looks.

  • Clarity first

    Can someone tell what this is, and what to do next, in five seconds?

  • Specific words

    Real prices, real names, real verbs. Vague copy is a design problem.

  • Fast, then careful

    AI for momentum. Human judgment for every detail that ships.

  • For everyone

    Readable contrast, keyboard paths and motion you can pause are part of the job.
